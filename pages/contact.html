What is an FAQ section?

An FAQ section can be used to quickly answer common questions about your business like "Where do you ship to?", "What are your opening hours?", or "How can I book a service?".

Why do FAQs matter?

FAQs help visitors find clear answers right away, reduce repetitive support messages, and build trust before they contact you.

Where can I add my FAQs?

You can place an FAQ section on your Contact page, product pages, or a dedicated Help page so users can reach it easily.
